[h2]🌌 關於《輓歌》 / About VesperMod[/h2]

[b]我們希望讓 Terraria 的戰鬥更有深度，讓探索重新充滿未知。[/b]

《輓歌》是一項大型內容擴充計畫，致力於重新探索職業、武器、飾品、坐騎與世界環境的可能性。

我們追求的不只是更多裝備與 Boss，而是具有獨特玩法的職業、值得主動使用的裝備、充滿秘密的環境，以及依靠機制而非單純堆疊數值構成的挑戰。

[b]我們希望玩家即使多次通關，依然能找到新的玩法與值得探索的事物。[/b]

[b]Our goal is to make Terraria's combat more engaging and its world worth exploring again.[/b]

VesperMod is an ambitious content expansion project exploring new possibilities for classes, weapons, accessories, mounts, and environments.

Rather than simply adding more equipment and bosses, we aim to create distinct playstyles, meaningful active abilities, environments filled with discoveries, and challenging encounters built around mechanics rather than inflated stats.

[b]We want every new playthrough to offer something worth discovering.[/b]

[b]以上為開發願景，並非目前版本已實裝的功能。[/b]

[b]These are development goals, not features currently available in the beta.[/b]

[h2]Beta 0.2.1 更新 / Update[/h2]

本版加入閃避翻滾、Poka 坐騎與猩紅地形敵人高瞰者，並更新職業起源頁及資源介面。蘑菇法杖與小蘑菇暫時移除，既有孢夢森林已移除，環境與貼圖預計重製。

This build adds dodge rolls, the Poka mount, and the Highwatcher in the Crimson, and updates the Origin page and resource displays. The mushroom staff and Baby Mushroom are temporarily removed. The previous Spore Dream Forest has been removed, with a redesigned environment and redrawn textures planned.
